Common questions

Is QuitBeacon medical advice?

No. QuitBeacon provides general wellness support and coping tools. It is not a substitute for a qualified healthcare professional, medication, or treatment plan.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for the current beta. Your quit plan, logs, routines, and preferences stay on your device.

How do reminders work?

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can configure them inside the app or disable notifications through your device settings.

What if I smoke after I planned not to?

Log what happened without judgment, notice the pattern, and start with the next moment. A slip does not erase the progress you made before it.

Where can I get urgent help?

QuitBeacon is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number. For help quitting, speak with a healthcare professional or a tobacco cessation service in your country.
