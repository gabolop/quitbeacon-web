The QuitBeacon approach

Quitting is a series of moments, not one perfect decision.

The hardest part can be the five minutes between an urge and what happens next. QuitBeacon is designed for that small window: notice what is happening, make room to breathe, and choose your next move.

We believe useful support should feel human, private, and possible to return to after a hard day.
